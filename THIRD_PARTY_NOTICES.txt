Credit SDSS-IV and the MaNGA, NSA and GZ3D authors and citizen scientists.

Galaxy Zoo Builder / published SpArcFiRe catalog
Derived pitch-comparison rows identify public galaxies and cite the Galaxy
Builder reference analyses (Lingard et al.2020,2021) and Hart et al.2017's
published SpArcFiRe measurements. The public repository contains derived
numerical comparison rows and citations; it does not redistribute the author
MATLAB implementation, MATLAB runtime, raw serialized author aggregates or
full author catalogs. The comparison uses different arm/projection aggregation
conventions and is not a newly executed SpArcFiRe run.
https://arxiv.org/abs/2006.10450
https://arxiv.org/abs/2105.04500
https://arxiv.org/abs/1708.04628

SPARC / calibrated kinematic-distance tables
Finite-halo and BTFR diagnostic tables derive from public SPARC data and
published velocity/error tables. Cite Lelli, McGaugh & Schombert2016, the
Lelli et al.2019 BTFR data and the relevant original rotation-curve observations
when reusing a galaxy's measurements. The established distance-calibration
framework and model assumptions are recorded in the model metadata, data card
and numerical implementation.
https://astroweb.case.edu/SPARC/
https://arxiv.org/abs/1606.09251
https://arxiv.org/abs/2006.08615
